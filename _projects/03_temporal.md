**Temporal action localization** aims to detect when actions start and end within untrimmed videos.

I research **weakly supervised temporal action localization (WTAL)**, where training uses video-level action labels instead of dense boundary annotations. Topics of interest include action proposal confidence, reliability-guided selection, and temporal boundary refinement.

Unpublished manuscripts and experimental results are intentionally not included here.
