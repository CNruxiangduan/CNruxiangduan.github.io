I contributed to **LiveVVT**, a collaborative research project exploring real-time and temporally consistent video virtual try-on.

The publicly available preprint studies a streaming diffusion design that preserves local bidirectional interactions using bounded look-ahead, together with temporal and global appearance memory.

**Paper:** [LiveVVT: High-Fidelity Video Virtual Try-On in Real Time (arXiv:2608.26714)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26714)
