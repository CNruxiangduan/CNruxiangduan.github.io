I investigate **human action recognition from 3D skeletal motion**, with a focus on structured relationships across joints and body parts.

My ongoing work explores hand-part relations, hypergraph-based higher-order interactions, and retrieval-enhanced motion representations.

**Key topics:** skeleton sequences, spatiotemporal graph learning, higher-order relations, and motion retrieval.

Unpublished manuscripts and experimental results are intentionally not included here.
