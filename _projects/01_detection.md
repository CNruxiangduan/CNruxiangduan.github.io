My research explores **efficient small-object detection** under low contrast, background clutter, and challenging object scales.

**Selected publications**

- [Focal Spotter, IEEE TGRS (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2025.3584812): feature focalization within and across scales.
- [Query-Calibrated Attention and Visibility-Aware Anisotropic Regression, Neurocomputing (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2026.134465): query attention calibration and progressive bounding-box regression.

Research interests include transformer detection, multi-scale representation, and tiny-object localization.
